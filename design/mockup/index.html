Learning Sciences & Technologies · Penn GSE

Learning with wonder. Creating with the world.

I’m Bodong Chen, an associate professor at the University of Pennsylvania Graduate School of Education. I study how people learn and create together, and design the pedagogies, analytics, and technologies that help them do it.

Publications
